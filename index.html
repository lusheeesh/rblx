How to get an account
Read this once — takes 30 seconds
1
Browse & pick an account
Scroll through the vault and find an account with the games you want. Click the card to see the full game list.
2
Click a link button
Each account has a Work.ink or Linkvertise button. Click either one — both lead to the same account details.
3
Complete the short task
You'll be shown a quick ad or task. Complete it — this is how the site stays free. It usually takes 15–30 seconds.
4
Get the credentials
After completing the task you'll see the username and password.
